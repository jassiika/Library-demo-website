Reading is better when it is shared.

Greenwood Public Library opened in 1984 with a single room and a few hundred donated books. Today it holds more than twelve thousand titles across six sections, and it is still run on the same idea: knowledge should be free to borrow.

Our librarians help with everything from a first library card to a deep research question. Children learn to read in our story corners, students prepare for exams in the study hall, and retirees return every week for the book club.

The library is also a place to meet. Author talks, summer reading programmes and archive open days bring the neighbourhood together, and every event is free to attend.

Whether you stay for five minutes or all afternoon, you are welcome. Bring a book, borrow one, or just enjoy the quiet.
